# Original-record retirement and convergence

The common portable-folder writer replaces the prototype ordinary-folder writer.
Refuse unfinished older stores and direct them to the explicit offline upgrade;
do not silently open a second writer. Provenance and retirement receipt readers
remain necessary for aliases, deletion protection and projection rebuild.

## Explicit adoption pairs

Do not infer duplicates from headers or shared MIME hashes. Require an exact
reviewed original/survivor report and verify byte identity. Preserve the visible
survivor's current ID, folder and state. Persist original provenance and a
retirement receipt into its portable occurrence before deleting the source row.
Flush the journal and rebuild a disposable projection to prove aliases survive
and originals do not reappear. Independent copies remain separate.

## Converted outgoing originals

Use actual outgoing state. Refuse retirement when an active draft/reference,
visible adopted copy, changed identity/receipt or portable binding needs retention.
Apply only the reviewed list; retain conversion receipts and deletion markers.
Retirement must not create, queue or send mail or sweep shared raw blobs.

## Backup and evidence

Live cleanup requires reviewed scope and backup policy. Explicit reuse of an
existing checked backup differs from making or validating a fresh backup.
Report omitted global checks honestly. Verify selected MIME/state, aliases,
receipts and journal completion; compare unrelated state once when required.

Live folder names, original IDs, outgoing counts, inventories and deployment
diaries belong outside Git. Converted files alone do not authorize deleting old
files or raw cache. Cache cleanup must classify references and protect drafts,
queued mail and unconverted/connected messages.
